Reflection-Based Learning

Understand concepts deeply by reflecting on your learning

Lesson Overview

Reflection-based learning focuses on thinking about what you have learned, how you learned it, and how it connects to prior knowledge. This improves long-term understanding and confidence.

Key Points

  • Learning is more effective with reflection
  • Self-questioning improves retention
  • Confidence tracking supports growth
